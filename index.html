Maya's workflow · Premiere Mobile

Opening — Meet Maya

This is Maya. She's been a content creator for about four years. She uses the same editing app she's always used. She knows where the tools are, which effects she likes and how to get the results she wants. And through the content she creates, she earns money.

Scene 1 — The audience insight

Why change what works?

MARIIA"Would you love to change your editing app?"
MAYA"No. When your workflow already works, why change it?"

She's used to this. She knows how everything works. Learning a completely new app means starting over, and she's not sure she needs to.

Then a Premiere Mobile icon appears, but Maya stays with her existing project.

This introduces the first important barrier: familiarity and the perception that other apps might be complicated. Creators are comfortable with what they already know. A new tool can feel like something they have to learn all over again.

Scene 2 — The market insight

Maya doesn't have time to start over

MARIIA"Why not try something new? Maybe another editing app could help."
MAYA"I already have so much to do. I don't have time to learn everything again."

Creating content comes with constant responsibilities. Maya needs to plan, film, edit and publish while keeping up with deadlines and her audience.

And Maya isn't alone. Kolsquare's 2025 research found that work-related stress is common among established creators.

MARIIA"So, learning a new app feels like another thing on your to-do list?"
MAYA"Exactly. I need something that fits into my routine."
Scene 3 — The Adobe insight

Maybe it's not what Maya expected

MARIIA"Have you ever tried Premiere Mobile?"
MAYA"Adobe? Isn't that difficult to use? I thought it was more for professionals."

Perceptions before testing (Focus Group, qualitative research). These represent perceptions from the focus group, not facts about Premiere Mobile.

MARIIA"Why don't we take a look?"

During our focus group, participants found Premiere Mobile cleaner and easier to use than they had expected. They appreciated its precision, layer management and export quality.

MAYA"Wait... that's actually easier than I thought."
MARIIA"So, would you switch?"
MAYA"Maybe. But how would it fit into the way I already work?"

A positive first impression can make creators curious. But trying a new app doesn't automatically mean changing the tools they rely on every day.

Scene 4 — The ending

Make room for a new way to create

MARIIA"Maybe trying something new doesn't mean changing everything."

Maya doesn't need to forget everything she already knows. She needs a tool that feels easy to use and can become part of the way she creates.

Final insight

Creators are afraid to disrupt a workflow that already works for them. They need a reason to believe that trying something new can feel natural, easy and worth their time.

How might we…

How might we make Premiere Mobile an easy and natural addition to creators' existing workflows?
